01. Counter-Strike 2

Genre: Tactical FPS / Competitive Shooter

Story

CS2 is primarily a competitive multiplayer game rather than a story-driven campaign. Two teams compete through objective-based rounds.

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Movement
  • Mouse — Aim
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • Right Click — Secondary fire
  • R — Reload
  • Shift — Walk
  • Ctrl — Crouch
  • Space — Jump
  • E — Interact

Controller

Controller support is available, but keyboard and mouse are generally used for competitive PC play.

PC or Laptop?

Both. It can run on relatively modest hardware, making it suitable for many gaming laptops and PCs.

Hardware Requirement
CPU Intel Core i5-750 or equivalent 4-thread CPU
GPU DirectX 11-compatible GPU with at least 1 GB VRAM
RAM 8 GB minimum

02. VALORANT

Genre: Tactical FPS

Story

VALORANT takes place in a futuristic world where agents with different abilities compete in tactical matches.

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Movement
  • Mouse — Aim
  • Left Click — Fire
  • R — Reload
  • Shift — Walk
  • Ctrl — Crouch
  • Number keys — Abilities / weapons

Controller

PC controller support is not the normal competitive control method. Keyboard and mouse are the standard setup.

PC or Laptop?

Both. It is relatively lightweight and can work well on many laptops.

Hardware Requirement
CPU Intel i3-540 / AMD Athlon 200GE
GPU Intel HD 4000 / Radeon R5 220
RAM 4 GB minimum

03. Grand Theft Auto V

Genre: Open-World Action / Adventure

Story

GTA V follows Michael, Franklin and Trevor as their lives become connected through crime, missions and conflicts in Los Santos.

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Movement
  • Mouse — Aim
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • F — Enter / exit vehicle
  • R — Reload
  • Space — Jump / handbrake
  • E — Interact

Controller

Full controller gameplay is supported.

PC or Laptop?

Both, but a gaming PC generally gives more thermal headroom and upgrade options.

Hardware Requirement
CPU Enhanced minimum: Intel i7-4770 / AMD FX-9590
GPU Enhanced minimum: GTX 1630 / RX 6400
RAM 8 GB minimum; 16 GB recommended

04. Cyberpunk 2077

Genre: Open-World Action RPG

Story

You play as V, a mercenary navigating Night City while becoming involved with powerful corporations, gangs and a digital personality connected to your mind.

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Movement
  • Mouse — Aim
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • R — Reload
  • E — Interact
  • Shift — Sprint
  • Ctrl — Crouch

Controller

Full controller support.

PC or Laptop?

A powerful gaming PC is generally more comfortable for high settings. A powerful gaming laptop can also run it.

Hardware Requirement
CPU Core i7-6700 / Ryzen 5 1600 minimum
GPU GTX 1060 6 GB / RX 580 8 GB / Arc A380 minimum
RAM 12 GB minimum

05. Minecraft

Genre: Sandbox / Survival

Story

Minecraft doesn't have one fixed story. Players explore, collect resources, build structures, fight enemies and can eventually reach the End dimension.

Keyboard & Mouse

  • WASD — Movement
  • Mouse — Look
  • Left Click — Attack / break
  • Right Click — Place / use
  • E — Inventory
  • Space — Jump
  • Shift — Sneak

Controller

Controller support depends on the edition and platform.

PC or Laptop?

Both. Java Edition can work on many systems, but shaders and high render distances can require much stronger hardware.

Hardware Requirement
CPU Modern entry-level CPU for basic play
GPU Integrated graphics for basic configurations
RAM More RAM is useful for large modpacks
